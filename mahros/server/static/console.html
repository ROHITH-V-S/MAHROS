Bed manager

what the winning hospital sees

When MAHROS picks a hospital, a bed there is held for the patient. Before the patient moves, the hospital's bed manager confirms the bed is really ready. If it is not (broken equipment, not cleaned yet, no nurse), they decline: the bed is freed and the patient is offered to the other hospitals, with the clock still running.

You are the bed manager at —

Applies to beds held from the moment you switch it on. The patient goes to the next hospital, recorded as “no response”. After 3 in a row it stops passing on: nobody is answering, and a coordinator has to phone.

Are they telling the truth?

the experiment

Accepting a transfer costs a hospital a bed. The cheapest way out is to say “sorry, we’re full”. Make a hospital protective below, then run the same case twice — once with checking off, once on.

Off = an ordinary auction: every “no” is taken at face value.

A protective hospital only lies when it actually has a bed. If the network is very full its refusals are simply true — turn the busyness down to see one caught.
